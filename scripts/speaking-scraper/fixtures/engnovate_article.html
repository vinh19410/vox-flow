IELTS Speaking Part 2: Describe a useful website you often visit

IELTS Speaking Part 2, Band 7.5, Technology

“Describe a website that you find very useful.”

Sample 1

One website that I rely on almost daily is Wikipedia because it gives concise background on unfamiliar topics.

Whenever I prepare for a presentation, I start there to map out key terms and verify dates.
